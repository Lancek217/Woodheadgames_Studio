Privacy Policy
Auto Empire 2: Car Dealer

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Developer: Woodhead Games · Contact: woodheadgamestudio@gmail.com · Website: woodheadgames.co.za
App: Auto Empire 2: Car Dealer (Android package name com.woodheadgames.autoempire2)

This policy explains what information Auto Empire 2 handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game keeps your progress on your device, shows ads through Google AdMob, and sells optional items through Google Play. Woodhead Games does not run accounts or servers for the game and does not collect your personal information.

1. Game progress and settings on your device

The game saves your progress, your settings (sound, haptics, text size, zoom) and your permanent purchases in the app's local storage on your phone. This data never leaves your device through the game, and Woodhead Games cannot see it. The app is set not to back its data up to your Google account.

To delete it, use Reset Game in the game (this resets progress but keeps permanent purchases), or uninstall the app to remove everything it stored.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads (you choose to watch them for a reward) and interstitial ads (full-screen ads shown at natural breaks in play). Ads are served by Google AdMob, Google's advertising service.

Google's advertising SDK in the app receives technical information needed to serve ads, such as your advertising ID, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google uses this under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Before the app requests any ads, it checks your consent status with Google's User Messaging Platform. In regions where Google requires consent for ads (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), a consent form appears. If you do not give consent where it is required, the app does not request ads for that session. Whether ads are personalised depends on the choices you make in that form.

You can change your ad choices at any time: open Settings and tap "Ad privacy options". This button appears only in regions where Google requires that you can change your choices.

Players who own the VIP Ad-Free Pass do not see interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are only shown when you ask for them.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional items are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and keeps the purchase record. Woodhead Games does not receive your payment card details or billing address.

The game receives from Google Play whether you own a purchase, and keeps permanent purchases (the VIP Ad-Free Pass and the other one-time perk) on your device. Consumable purchases are used up in the game.

To restore a permanent purchase after reinstalling or changing phones, use Restore purchases in the game's shop. Purchases, refunds and billing questions are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and policies.

4. Crash and performance information (Google Play)

Woodhead Games does not add a crash-reporting or analytics SDK to the game. Crash and performance reports reach the developer only through Google Play vitals, which Google collects from devices whose owners have chosen to share usage and diagnostic data with Google and developers. Google's policy applies to that data.

5. Fonts

The game uses the fonts already installed on your device. It does not load fonts from Google or any other website.

6. Information we do not collect

The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no location access, no access to your contacts or photos, and no chat or social features. Woodhead Games does not sell personal information.

7. Children

Auto Empire 2 is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. Ad requests are set to a general audience, and Woodhead Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Your choices and requests

  • Ad choices: Settings, "Ad privacy options" (where Google requires it), or your device's ad settings.
  • Game data: Reset Game in the game, or uninstall the app.
  • Purchase records: Google Play.
  • Ad data held by Google: Google's own privacy controls.
  • Anything Woodhead Games holds, such as emails you sent us: email woodheadgamestudio@gmail.com. We hold no account or game-progress data on our servers, so there is usually nothing to delete on our side.

9. Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting new information, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the "Last updated" date will change.
