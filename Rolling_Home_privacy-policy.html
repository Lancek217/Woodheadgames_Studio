Privacy Policy
Rolling Home: RV Survival

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Developer: Woodhead Games · Contact: woodheadgamestudio@gmail.com · Website: woodheadgames.co.za
App: Rolling Home: RV Survival (Android package name com.woodheadgames.roadtripsurvival)

This policy explains what information Rolling Home handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: the game keeps your progress on your device, shows ads through Google AdMob, and sells optional items through Google Play. Woodhead Games does not run accounts or servers for the game and does not collect your personal information.

1. Game progress and settings on your device

The game saves your progress, your settings (sound, controls, display options) and any permanent purchases in the app's local storage on your phone. The game does not send this data to Woodhead Games, and Woodhead Games cannot see it. If you use Android's device backup, Android may include the app's data in your Google account backup, under Google's own terms.

To delete it, uninstall the app, or clear the app's storage in your phone's Android settings. Either removes everything the game stored on your device.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded ads (you choose to watch them for a reward) and interstitial ads (full-screen ads). Ads are served by Google AdMob, Google's advertising service.

Google's advertising SDK in the app receives technical information needed to serve ads, such as your advertising ID, device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google uses this under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Before the app requests any ads, it checks your consent status with Google's User Messaging Platform. In regions where Google requires consent for ads (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states), a consent form appears. If you do not give consent where it is required, the app does not request ads for that session. Whether ads are personalised depends on the choices you make in that form.

You can change your ad choices at any time through the ad privacy options the game offers in regions where Google requires them, and through your device's Google ad settings.

Where the game offers an ad-removal purchase, players who buy it do not see interstitial ads. Rewarded ads are only shown when you ask for them.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional items are sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment and keeps the purchase record. Woodhead Games does not receive your payment card details or billing address.

The game receives from Google Play whether you own a purchase, and keeps permanent purchases on your device. Consumable purchases are used up in the game.

Permanent purchases can be restored after reinstalling or changing phones through Google Play. Purchases, refunds and billing questions are handled by Google Play under Google's own terms and policies.

4. Crash and performance information (Google Play)

Woodhead Games does not add a crash-reporting or analytics SDK to the game. Crash and performance reports reach the developer only through Google Play vitals, which Google collects from devices whose owners have chosen to share usage and diagnostic data with Google and developers. Google's policy applies to that data.

5. Our other games list

The game's "Our other games" page loads a short list of Woodhead Games titles from woodheadgames.co.za and keeps a copy on your device so it can show the list offline. Like any request to a website, this lets the website host see your device's IP address. The game sends no advertising ID, no game data and no personal information in this request. Tapping a game opens its Google Play page outside the game.

6. Fonts and other web content

The game uses the fonts already installed on your device and the files bundled inside the app. It does not load fonts from Google or any other website.

7. Information we do not collect

The game has no user accounts, no sign-in, no analytics SDK, no location access, no access to your contacts or photos, and no chat or social features. Woodhead Games does not sell personal information.

8. Children

Rolling Home is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not designed for children. Ad requests are set to a general audience, and Woodhead Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Your choices and requests

  • Ad choices: the game's ad privacy options (where Google requires them), or your device's Google ad settings.
  • Game data: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in Android settings.
  • Purchase records: Google Play.
  • Ad data held by Google: Google's own privacy controls.
  • Anything Woodhead Games holds, such as emails you sent us: email woodheadgamestudio@gmail.com. We hold no account or game-progress data on our servers, so there is usually nothing to delete on our side.

10. Changes to this policy

If the game starts collecting new information, this policy will be updated before the change ships, and the "Last updated" date will change.
